Ali Aboutalebiyan Profile

AI, Control Systems & Industrial Project Coordination

My main focus is designing and implementing industrial automation using computer vision and AI—from product inspection and defect detection to controlling equipment movement. I combine camera images with sensor data to understand the operating environment, monitor equipment, and develop ways to detect and manage faults. A key goal is to identify declining performance and early signs of failure before equipment stops working, allowing timely action to reduce the risk of production downtime and the resulting losses.

I also define the scope of work, plan the schedule, and coordinate specialists. I work with trusted partners for PLC programming, SCADA monitoring and control systems, and industrial networks.
